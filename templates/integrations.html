What am I looking at?

1 · EdVidura (always)

Quiz scores, lesson progress, and Ask Vidura turns saved for this school. Open Activity or Class results — easiest place for day-to-day teaching.

2 · Charts (Metabase)

Pretty graphs over the same school data (attempts, daily activity counts). Good for overview — not for grading. Moodle still owns the official gradebook.

3 · Learning history (Yet)

Optional archive of every learning event (“statement”). Use when you need a detailed trail — not for everyday class checks.
