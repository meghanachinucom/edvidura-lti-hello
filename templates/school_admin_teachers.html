Teachers

Teacher accounts are created in Moodle, not EdVidura. When a teacher opens this tool via LTI, EdVidura uses their Moodle role and course — no separate EdVidura login.

Open Moodle users Link classes & content

How it works

  1. In Moodle: create the teacher, enroll them in the course.
  2. In EdVidura: bind that Moodle course to a class + curriculum (Classes).
  3. Teacher launches EdVidura from Moodle → sees that class’s content and results.
