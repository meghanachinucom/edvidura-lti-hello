EdVidura
Simple overview

A classroom inside Moodle — not a second Moodle.

  • Students open it from their Moodle course, like any other activity.
  • They get lessons, a quiz, and an AI tutor for that class.
  • The quiz mark goes back to Moodle. Moodle stays the gradebook.

The core idea

Who does what

Moodle keeps

  • Student login and class list
  • Who is the teacher
  • The official gradebook
  • The course itself

EdVidura adds

  • The lesson path for the class
  • Practice and the checkpoint quiz
  • The AI tutor (Ask Vidura)
  • A record of what the student did

For students

What a student does

  • Log in to Moodle and open the EdVidura activity.
  • Follow the lessons for their class.
  • Take a short quiz to check understanding.
  • Ask Vidura for a hint if they are stuck — it will not write the exam for them.
  • See their progress. The quiz score is sent back to Moodle.
EdVidura03 / 10
For teachers

What a teacher does

  • Open the same tool from Moodle as a teacher.
  • See who finished the quiz and who is struggling.
  • Use AI to draft quiz questions, then check them before they go live.
  • Get suggested next steps — they still decide what to do.
  • Enrol students in Moodle, not in EdVidura.

Moodle connection

How it plugs into Moodle

  • It is a standard course tool (LTI). Schools already know this pattern.
  • A student click in Moodle opens EdVidura in a new window.
  • We can see who is in the class so the right people get the right work.
  • When they finish a quiz, the score is sent back to Moodle’s gradebook.
  • Each school’s data is kept separate. Unknown schools cannot get in.
EdVidura05 / 10
AI tutor

Ask Vidura, in plain words

  • A tutor for this class — not a general chatbot for the whole internet.
  • It answers from the teacher’s approved notes and books, and shows where the answer came from.
  • It hints and explains. It will not write the assignment or the answer key.
  • It can speak and listen in English and several Indian languages.
  • If no AI key is set, a simple local helper still works for demos.
EdVidura06 / 10
AI and grades

AI never writes the gradebook

AI may

  • Draft quiz questions from a lesson or PDF
  • Help a teacher write a lesson
  • Suggest a mark on a written answer
  • Suggest what to teach next

AI must not

  • Put a mark into Moodle by itself
  • Use random websites as the textbook
  • Do the student’s exam for them
  • Replace the teacher’s decision
