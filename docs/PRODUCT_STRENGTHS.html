EdVidura

Strengths & capabilities

EdVidura is an LTI learning layer that sits inside Moodle (or another LMS). It adds practice, coaching, handbooks, and section diagnostics while the LMS stays the system of record for grades and enrolment.

Production App edvidura-app Updated Nov 2026

Core strengths

What makes the product solid for real classrooms and multi-tenant use.

  • LMS-native, not LMS-replacing — launches from Moodle via LTI 1.3 Advantage; official gradebook and roster stay in the LMS.
  • Tenant isolation — each school or organisation is separated at the database with row-level security.
  • Clear classroom UI — CampusHub layout with breathing room: one job per section, short copy, fewer competing actions.
  • Handbook-grounded AI — Ask Vidura gives Socratic hints from approved manuals, not quiz answers.
  • Evidence-linked skills — proficiency levels tied to quizzes, labs, and other assessment evidence.
  • Section-scoped teaching tools — results, sync, and diagnostics for this class — not a district SIS.

Learner capabilities

Inside the course tool after LTI launch.

  • Course home with progress and next step
  • Lesson and lab continuum with prerequisites
  • Checkpoint quizzes with instant feedback
  • Ask Vidura study coach (cited sources)
  • Class manuals / PeBL handbook reader
  • My Progress: roadmap, skills, gaps, goals
  • Spatial / WebXR practice labs where enabled
  • Multi-language UI for key handbook surfaces

Teacher capabilities

Built for day-to-day section teaching.

  • Class results and Moodle grade sync (AGS)
  • Diagnostics: stuck topics, activity, xAPI, gaps
  • Assign short remediation drills
  • Difference training vs skill targets
  • Competency mapping (CAT / TO → skills)
  • DCT lesson planner & SME source registry
  • Preview student view from the gradebook

Platform capabilities

Architecture that supports school, corporate, and government segments.

  • LTI Advantage — launch, Dynamic Registration onboarding, Assignment and Grade Services passback.
  • Adaptive learning — gap / difference paths and personalised next activities.
  • SME & handbooks — approved manuals indexed for the coach; public query API and ETIMS webhook for content sync.
  • xAPI middleware — statements from quizzes, labs, and learning events for analytics.
  • Events & receipts — outbox-style event envelope and sealed grade attempt evidence.
  • Segment packs — e.g. government Pravachana video library alongside school STEM courses.
  • Ops & tenancy — onboarding UI, capability flags per tenant, security boot checks.

Experience polish (current UI)

Screens designed to be easy to understand in demos and daily use.

  • Student home — calm dashboard, clear next action.
  • Progress — roadmap, skills, gaps, and goals as simple cards.
  • Ask Vidura — focused chat; sources and language in a light bar.
  • Lessons & manuals — one primary CTA; offline-friendly handbooks.
  • Quiz & XR — checkpoint focus; lean lab cards to launch.
  • Teacher results & diagnostics — short heroes, plain tabs, scannable tables.

Boundary with the LMS

Keeps procurement and trust conversations clean.

  • Enrolment and the official gradebook remain in Moodle (or Canvas).
  • EdVidura is the practice, coaching, handbook, and diagnostics layer.
  • Teachers see sync status before and after grade passback.
